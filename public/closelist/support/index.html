Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add guests?

Allow contacts access to import people, or use the add-person button to enter guests manually. Contacts access is optional.

Does CloseList send messages automatically?

No. CloseList opens WhatsApp with a prepared message. You choose whether to send it, then confirm the result when you return to CloseList. WhatsApp must be installed and available for WhatsApp invitations.

What is free, and what does Unlimited cost?

In version 1.3, the first 50 confirmed sends are free. Completing an optional rewarded ad unlocks another block of 50. CloseList Unlimited costs ₹499 as a one-time purchase in India and removes future ad breaks. It is not a subscription. Other countries use the price shown in the App Store purchase screen.

How do I restore Unlimited?

Sign in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Open Go Premium and choose Restore purchase. An internet connection is required. If it does not restore, email support with the error message; do not send your Apple password or payment-card details.

Why is an ad unavailable?

Ad availability depends on your region, connection, and available advertising. Try again later. Additional sends are granted only after the rewarded ad completes successfully.

How do I manage advertising privacy?

Open Settings → Ad privacy choices when that option is available. Read the privacy policy for details about Google AdMob.

How do I remove my data?

Delete individual guests or use Settings → Reset App. Export anything you want to keep first. Backups and copies you previously shared may remain. Removing app data does not refund an Apple purchase.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles purchase and refund requests. Visit https://reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple Account used to buy Unlimited.

Where is my guest list stored?

Entirely on your iPhone. CloseList has no servers and no account system — your contacts, notes, and invitation history are never uploaded anywhere. That also means deleting the app deletes your list, so export a copy first if you're switching phones.
