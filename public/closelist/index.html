Work through your list, one swipe at a time

No messy spreadsheets, no scattered napkins, no forgotten uncles. Just a calm, structured workflow that gets the job done.

1

Import Contacts

Import your phone contacts into one central triage queue. You can also add guests manually if you don't grant contacts access.

2

Swipe to Triage

Sort contacts instantly with intuitive gestures: swipe right to shortlist for invitations, left to skip. Work through hundreds in minutes.

3

Invite Efficiently

Select guests from your "To-Invite" queue. Make a call directly or send pre-filled WhatsApp templates containing custom placeholders.

4

Honest Check-In

CloseList checks in after every invitation action to verify if they were actually reached. Nobody gets marked "invited" by accident.
